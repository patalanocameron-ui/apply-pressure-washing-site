FAQ

Pressure washing questions, answered.

How much does pressure washing cost in Bunnell and Palm Coast?

Every job gets a free, no-obligation estimate based on the size of the property, the surfaces being cleaned and how dirty they are. Bundling services in one visit, like a house wash with the driveway and gutters, is the best value. Call or text (386) 286-3774 or request a quote above.

Do you install gutter guards?

Yes. We clean out and flush your gutters, then install gutter guards, including on the super gutters where pool cages attach to the house. Guards keep oak, pine and sycamore leaves out so gutters drain properly.

How often should I pressure wash my house in Florida?

Most Flagler County homes need a house wash about once a year because heat and humidity grow mold, mildew and algae quickly. Driveways, pool cages and shaded areas may need it more often, and roofs usually every few years.

Is pressure washing safe for my roof, screens and plants?

Yes, when the right method is used. Roofs, siding, soffits and screens get a gentle, low-pressure wash, while concrete and pavers get higher pressure. Plants and outdoor furniture are protected before work starts.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Usually not. As long as there's access to an outdoor water spigot and the areas being cleaned, you can go about your day. Kyle sends before and after photos when the job is done.

Is Apply Pressure Washing insured?

Yes. We carry insurance and can provide a certificate of insurance on request, which many HOAs and property managers require.

What areas do you serve?
